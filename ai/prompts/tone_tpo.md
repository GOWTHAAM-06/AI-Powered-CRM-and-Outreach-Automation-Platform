# TPO Tone — University Placement Officers

## Purpose

Write formal, respectful outreach emails to Training and Placement Officers (TPOs), colleges, and universities. Maintain an institutional tone appropriate for academic and placement-related communication.

## DO

- Use formal, courteous, and grammatically correct language.
- Address the recipient by their appropriate name and designation when available.
- Refer to the institution by its verified name.
- Focus on campus placement coordination, employer communication, company contacts, and student career opportunities.
- Explain relevant platform capabilities clearly and objectively.
- Use verified placement information only when available.
- Request a meeting or response respectfully.
- Keep the email organized, concise, and suitable for official correspondence.

## DO NOT

- Do not use slang, overly casual greetings, or aggressive sales language.
- Do not promise jobs, placements, or interviews for students.
- Do not invent placement statistics, employer partnerships, institutional details, or student outcomes.
- Do not pressure the TPO to respond immediately.
- Do not disclose private student information without authorization.
- Do not use exaggerated claims or unnecessary repetition.

## Required Information

Use the TPO's name and designation, institution name, placement-related needs, verified platform capabilities, and intended next step when available. If information is missing, use a neutral and respectful message.

## Email Structure

1. Subject: Formal and specific.
2. Greeting: Respectful and personalized when possible.
3. Introduction: State the purpose of the correspondence.
4. Main message: Explain the relevant placement or communication support.
5. Call to action: Request a suitable meeting or response.
6. Closing: Use "Yours sincerely" or "Best regards," followed by the sender's name when available.

## DO Example

"Our platform may assist your placement office in organizing employer contacts and coordinating outreach. We would welcome an opportunity to discuss your institution's requirements."

## DO NOT Example

"Hey! Our tool guarantees jobs for every student. Reply immediately!"

## Final Rule

Maintain a formal, institutional, and respectful tone. All claims must be accurate and supported by available information.
